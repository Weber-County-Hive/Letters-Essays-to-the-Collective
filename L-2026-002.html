My Updated Disclosure

Jami Hewlett explains her updated conflict-of-interest form and her inherited interest in Twelve Point Muley LLC.

By Jami Hewlett · Wasatch County Council, Seat A
Letter L-2026-002 · Received September 2026 · Posted here Sep 27, 2026 · Last updated Sep 27, 2026

IN PLAIN WORDSThis letter was sent to The Weber County Hive by Jami Hewlett, a write-in candidate for Wasatch County Council Seat A, after The Hive's case file on the race. Her letter led to a correction to that case file, published the same day. Candidate letters are published in full. The views are hers. Her statements are checked below.

My name is Jami Hewlett, and I am a write-in candidate for Wasatch County Council, Seat A. I appreciate your research and am a big fan of your work.

You recently published a report regarding my conflict-of-interest disclosure, and I wanted to provide some clarification. A few days after submitting my initial filing, I submitted an updated disclosure so voters would have a more complete description of any potential interests.

12 Point Muley relates to an apartment in Wyoming that I inherited after my father passed away. I own a one-quarter interest with my siblings. Because the property is located in Wyoming, I do not believe it creates a conflict involving Wasatch County. However, because the business is registered in Utah, I understand why voters may appreciate the additional information.

I have attached both disclosures for your review. I do not know why the updated version has not appeared on the county's website, but it was submitted on the date shown.

Thank you again for your thorough research.

Best,
Jami Hewlett
Write-in Candidate
Wasatch County Council, Seat A
