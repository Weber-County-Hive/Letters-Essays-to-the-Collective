Why I'm Running for Seat A

Rachel Kahler introduces her campaign, her record on the Heber City Council, and her view of her CAMS role.

By Rachel Kahler · Wasatch County Council, Seat A
Letter L-2026-001 · Received Sep 15, 2026 · Posted here Sep 27, 2026 · Last updated Sep 27, 2026

IN PLAIN WORDSThis letter was sent to The Weber County Hive by Rachel Kahler, the Republican nominee for Wasatch County Council Seat A. On Sept. 15, 2026, The Hive told her it would put her words online with the other candidates'. Candidate letters are published in full, above the usual 400-word limit, with typos fixed and her phone number removed. The views are hers. Statements about her own record are marked below as checked or not yet checked; nothing has been changed to match a record.

Hello,

I wish I knew your name.

As you seem very interested in my work and my campaign for Wasatch County Council, I’d love the opportunity to introduce myself, and perhaps you will share some information about you. I appreciated your post earlier today about getting involved in politics. I felt your words and meaning were true and sincere. I hope we might meet or have the opportunity to speak with each other by phone. I appreciate your perspective and hope you are open to understanding my position and desires to work towards a better community.

I am running for the Wasatch County Council at-large seat A because I believe deeply in thoughtful stewardship of the place we all call home. Wasatch County is growing, and how we guide that growth matters. My work in historic preservation, community building, and long-range planning has shown me that we can protect our valley’s character while still preparing responsibly for the future. I am committed to helping our communities grow with intention.

While serving four years on Heber City Council I was an advocate for reducing height and density, and successfully changed the code on Main Street from five stories to three stories, only allowing four stories when developers agreed to include attainable housing. Voted NO to the NVOZ, North Village Overlay Zone, I voted NO to the annexation request from Harvest Village, now known as The Slopes. I voted NO to the Wellberg annexation request. I voted Yes to the Jordanelle Ridge Master Development agreement. Let’s talk about WHY.

  • Support people-centered planning that protects livability, neighborhood character, local businesses and community gathering spaces.
  • I believe residents, cities and stakeholders should be involved early in major planning decisions.
  • I am an advocate for protecting historic buildings, neighborhoods and landscapes as community assets that strengthen our identity. And am actively pursuing opportunities to create a National Historic District, as well as continuing to add properties to the Heber Historic Tour.
  • I am an advocate for coordinated transportation and land-use planning that improves safety, reduces congestion and connects communities. I do support the implementation of the Heber Valley Bypass. This is not a secret. I support downtown businesses that need and deserve a Main Street that is a viable economic hub.
  • I support pedestrian safety, bicycle connectivity and accessible public transportation, particularly in town centers. I support citizens participating in helping to pay for public transportation with use fees for bus and van transportation.
  • I believe growth must be aligned with available water, roads, utilities and public services. All development must demonstrate paper and wet water rights, have utility plans that have passed through a strict review process with our utility partners, including HL&P, Utah Water Conservancy, Jordanelle Special Service District, Heber Valley Special Service District, Twin Creeks Special Service District, Wasatch Fire and EMS District, Wasatch County Sheriff Department and anyone that will be required to provide utility services to new development.
  • I expect developers to contribute fairly to the infrastructure required by their projects. Through impact fees, preservation funds, additional community amenities such as parks and public services.
  • I bring experience from two years on the North Village Special Service District and four years on the Heber Light & Power Board.
  • I am committed to collaboration, clear communication and fiscal responsibility in county government.
  • I believe decisions should be based on reliable data and long-term outcomes, not social media pressure or backroom politics.
  • I will work to strengthen trust among the county, cities, businesses and residents while ensuring public funds are invested wisely.
  • My approach: preserve what matters, strengthen our communities and plan responsibly for the future.

Wasatch County stands at an important crossroads. We can allow growth to happen to us, or we can shape it thoughtfully and responsibly. I believe in preserving what matters, strengthening community, and planning clearly for the future, and that is the approach I will bring to the Wasatch County Council.

As a candidate for the Wasatch County Council, I believe transparency regarding my professional roles is important. I serve as Executive Director of the Community Alliance for Main Street (CAMS), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to revitalizing downtown Heber City through historic preservation, economic development, beautification, and community-centered programming.

CAMS holds a three-year contract with Heber City to provide event management and related services. That agreement is between Heber City and CAMS as an independent nonprofit organization. The contract is not with me personally, nor do I personally hold the contract.

For that reason, I do not consider my employment with CAMS, by itself, to constitute a conflict of interest with service on the Wasatch County Council. It is, however, a professional relationship that I am fully willing to disclose in the interest of transparency.

Should a specific matter come before the County Council involving CAMS in a way that could create an actual or potential financial or professional conflict for me, I would disclose that relationship and follow all applicable County policies and Utah law regarding participation or recusal.

If you are really open to understanding and truth. Let’s chat. [Phone number removed by the editor.]

Rachel
